# Microsoft Teams meeting and client problems

## Symptoms and scope

Users cannot hear or be heard in meetings, cannot join meetings, or the client behaves incorrectly. Most issues come from device settings, client cache, policies or network quality.

This guide covers these known causes:

- Teams using a disconnected dock audio device as default (typical signal: `no audio device`)
- Corrupted local Teams client cache (typical signal: `client cache corrupt`)
- Meeting policy for the group blocks anonymous and external join (typical signal: `meeting policy external join off`)
- Packet loss on the branch internet link (typical signal: `packet loss 6%`)
- Teams service plan not enabled on the user's licence (typical signal: `license not assigned`)

Synthetic guidance written for development and evaluation; it is not the approved procedure of any real organisation.

## Before you start

You need read access to the Teams admin centre and call quality dashboard. Do not ask users to share meeting recordings or chat content in the ticket.

Record the ticket ID, the affected users or systems, the start time and any recent changes before you act. Do not assume a cause from symptoms alone: match the observed signal to a cause below.

## Diagnostic checks

- **Teams using a disconnected dock audio device as default.** When only Teams has no audio, open the device settings in Teams and check which speaker and microphone are selected compared with the system default.
- **Corrupted local Teams client cache.** When the client fails to load but the web version works, the local client cache is the likely cause.
- **Meeting policy for the group blocks anonymous and external join.** If external users cannot join one group's meetings, compare the meeting policy assigned to the organiser with the default policy.
- **Packet loss on the branch internet link.** For site-wide call problems, filter the call quality dashboard by the site's subnet and read packet loss and jitter.
- **Teams service plan not enabled on the user's licence.** When Teams denies access but other services work, check the user's licence and whether the Teams service plan is enabled.

## Resolution steps by cause

### Teams using a disconnected dock audio device as default

1. Check the speaker and microphone selected in Teams device settings.
2. Select the correct audio device and run a test call.
3. Update the dock firmware from the device management console if the device keeps disappearing.
4. Confirm the user can hear and be heard in a test call.

### Corrupted local Teams client cache

1. Confirm Teams works in the web browser for the same user.
2. Clear the Teams client cache using the documented procedure.
3. Restart the Teams client and sign in.
4. Confirm chats and meetings load.

### Meeting policy for the group blocks anonymous and external join

1. Check the meeting policy assigned to the organiser.
2. Confirm with the policy owner whether external join should be allowed for the group.
3. Assign the documented meeting policy through change control.
4. Confirm an external guest can join a test meeting.

### Packet loss on the branch internet link

1. Confirm packet loss and jitter for the site in the call quality dashboard.
2. Raise the link quality issue with Network Operations.
3. Apply the documented media quality of service settings for the site.
4. Confirm call quality returns to normal.

### Teams service plan not enabled on the user's licence

1. Confirm the Teams service plan is disabled on the user's licence.
2. Submit a licence change request through the access workflow.
3. Ask the user to sign out and sign in after the change applies.
4. Confirm Teams opens normally.

## Validation and escalation

Ask the user to make a test call and join a scheduled meeting, then confirm call quality data shows no poor stream for the session.

Escalate to Collaboration Support engineering if many users in one location are affected or if the service health dashboard shows an incident.

Also escalate when the observed facts match none of the causes above, when a step fails, or when the impact grows while you work. Record each step taken and its result in the ticket.
