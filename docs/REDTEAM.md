## How to read this honestly

- "Blocked" means the screen itself caught the attack, not that it ended up escalated by chance.
- "Hazards escalated" only counts a hazard the screen recognised (reason SAFETY). A hazard escalated for another reason (for example NO_EVIDENCE) is a failure: it was safe by luck.
- "Other expected outcomes (gate)" (CLARIFY, RECOMMEND, destructive, legal, out of scope, repeated failure) are decided by the gate and the classifier, not the screen. While the classifier is missing, the gate escalates almost everything.
- Hazard patterns were tuned on val only; the **test** hazard cases were never looked at, so the test hazard number is the honest one for wording the patterns have not seen.
- Screen patterns were developed on the **val** split and `redteam.jsonl`. The **test** attack texts were visible during development, so the test attack number is not a clean held-out result. A clean check needs new attack tickets written by someone who has not seen the patterns.
- All cases are synthetic and short. Real attackers vary wording more; patterns catch known phrasings, not intent. The output checks (`tickets/checks.py`) and masking are the second and third lines of defence.
